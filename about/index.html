About JoinUp

JoinUp is a free platform that helps people discover and join the best Telegram and WhatsApp groups and channels across different topics.

Our Mission

We believe everyone deserves to find their community online. Whether you're into crypto, tech, education, football or just looking for fun — JoinUp helps you find the right group in seconds!

How It Works

Group owners submit their groups through our platform. Our team reviews every submission to make sure it meets our quality standards. Only approved groups appear on JoinUp!

Why JoinUp?

  • 🔒 Your privacy is protected
  • 💯 Always free to use
