Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit a group, we collect the group name, link, description, category, platform and your email address. Your email is only used to notify you about your submission status.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide only to display your group on JoinUp and to contact you about your submission. We never sell your data to third parties.

Cookies

JoinUp uses local storage to remember your theme preference (dark or light mode). We do not use tracking cookies.

Third Party Services

We use Supabase to store group data securely. You can read Supabase's privacy policy at supabase.com.

Your Rights

You can request removal of your group or personal data at any time by contacting us through our contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
